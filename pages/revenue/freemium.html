Concept Overview

Free access builds trust before monetization.

Vitora's freemium model gives users free access to basic habit tracking, introductory nutrition tips and educational content. The goal is not to monetize every user immediately. Instead, free access helps Vitora build trust, increase product adoption and identify engaged users who may later upgrade to Vitora Plus.

Problem / Opportunity

Why free entry matters in digital health.

01

High Trust Barrier

Health and nutrition apps require trust before users are willing to pay for personal guidance.

02

User Acquisition Cost

A free entry model can reduce friction because users can test the product before committing financially.

03

Engagement-Based Conversion

Users who repeatedly track habits and see progress are more likely to understand the value of premium features.

Strategic Benefit

Freemium supports growth without forcing early payment.

  • Free access supports trust-building.
  • Freemium can grow the user base faster than paid-only access.
  • Premium conversion creates scalable digital revenue.
  • The model fits students and young professionals because they can try the app before paying.
