Concept Overview

Partner access instead of only direct-to-consumer sales.

Vitora B2B is a partner model for gyms, fitness chains, universities and corporate health programs. Instead of only selling directly to individual users, Vitora can license its AI-supported nutrition, habit and wellbeing platform to organizations. These partners can offer Vitora as a digital coaching layer inside their own membership or employee-benefit system.

Problem

Why partners need a digital coaching layer.

01

Low Member Retention

Many gym members stop training because they lack structure, feedback and motivation after the first weeks.

02

Limited Personalization

Trainers cannot manually personalize nutrition, habit and wellbeing guidance for every member at scale.

03

Disconnected Health Data

Training, nutrition and wellbeing information are often separated across different tools, making progress harder to understand.

B2B Solution

Vitora gives partners a scalable support system.

01

AI Coaching for Members

Members receive realistic habit, nutrition and reflection guidance connected to their personal goals.

02

Dashboard for Partners

Gyms and corporate health teams can understand engagement, adoption and progress signals without exposing sensitive private details.

03

Premium Upsell Logic

Partners can use Vitora to strengthen premium memberships, corporate wellbeing packages or student health programs.
