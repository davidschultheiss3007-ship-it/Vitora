Concept Overview

Vitora Plus turns sustained app usage into recurring revenue.

Vitora Plus is the paid subscription tier. Users pay a monthly fee for deeper AI recommendations, progress analytics and coach-supported guidance. This model creates predictable recurring revenue and funds product quality, coach support and long-term platform development.
