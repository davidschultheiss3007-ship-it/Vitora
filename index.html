The problem

People do not fail because of willpower. They fail because of the system.

Most users start with motivation, but their tools are fragmented. Training, nutrition and mental wellbeing usually happen in separate apps that do not learn from each other.

Users receive isolated data instead of connected guidance. Vitora turns that chaos into one clear feedback loop.
